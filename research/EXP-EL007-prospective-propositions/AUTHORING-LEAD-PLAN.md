# EXP-EL007 abstract authoring leads

**Status:** Mechanical preparation rule; no cases or labels.

Use the frozen `CC-BY-SCREEN-001.json` report only. Keep all 41 sources in
their existing order. For a source with distinct abstract paragraphs whose
section names match `Results` and `Conclusion` (allowing a trailing colon or
plural), take the first paragraph of each as an exact-text authoring lead.
The Conclusion paragraph is a possible author-authored claim location; the
Results paragraph is a possible evidence location. Do not summarize, split,
rewrite, or label either paragraph. Preserve each field path and text hash.
For all other sources, retain a manual-inspection queue record without a
pairing. Do not drop them or substitute another article.

This method identifies text locations, not suitable claims. The conclusion
may contain several propositions or may exceed what the selected Results
paragraph supports. Reviewers must inspect the complete article and decide
whether a bounded natural claim and exact passage can be admitted. The two
paragraphs come from the **same article**, so this is a within-source
relationship test and never independent corroboration. No semantic label,
proposition split, or JEV input is produced by this preparation step.
